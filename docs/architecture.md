# Architecture, as built

`docs/spec.md` is the plan. This page is what exists on `main`, checked against the code on 2026-10-20. Where the two differ, this page says "not yet". The reasons behind each part are in `docs/decisions/`.

## The picture today

```
apps/web (Vue 3 PWA) ──► apps/api (Fastify on Render) ──► Render Postgres (EU, PITR), with the job queue (pg-boss)
                                   │
                                   ├──► Scaleway Object Storage   documents' files (decision 0029)
                                   ├──► packages/ledger   converts the catalogue's packs, and posts documents into stored movements
                                   └──► packages/extract  reads documents in a background job (decision 0030)

packages/extract ──► packages/ledger (line totals, unit costs: decision 0016)
                 └─► LLM via OpenRouter: the API's job, and the eval command

ops/backup (Render cron, weekly) ──► Scaleway Object Storage (encrypted DB dumps)
```

Posting is a service (#116) with endpoints for delivery notes and reversals (#119), and invoices (#120). Staging and production each have their own documents' bucket and OpenRouter key: `render.yaml` names the bucket, and the keys are entered in Render's dashboard (README, "One-time setup").

What it read, the checks it failed, and each try's cost are stored for review. Posting (#116, decision 0034): a reviewed document's lines and the ledger's movements are stored in one audited transaction, under a lock that makes postings one at a time, and a posted document is reversed by a new posted document. The database refuses changing or deleting a movement, a posted line or a posted document. Movements are read back in the order they were posted (`sequence`), and database-level property tests check that they give the same stock and costs as `ledger`. Posting a delivery note (#119, decision 0038): any role, once nothing is left before posting it; office and owner also while its supplier is new. The matching is worked out again inside the transaction; each line is received at the pack of the supplier product it matched, its price per printed unit never divided, into the storeroom chosen for it or its product's default, and at €0 when it shows no price. A posted document's page reads its lines and movements, and its reversal's. The owner reverses a posted document, dated that day, unless an invoice that stands still bills it, and may then make a corrected copy of it, in review, shown with the original's files. Reversals themselves stay out of the documents list; a reversed document names its reversal. The duplicate check ignores reversed documents. Invoices (#120, decision 0041): each line bills a posted delivery-note line, or is received with the invoice. It's suggested among the delivery notes the invoice lists, or else those of the 62 days up to its date, when it's the only candidate on both sides. Office and the owner confirm each line (`invoice_line_bills`); a correction that adds or removes lines clears the choices. The matching answer gives a billed line's price now (the receipt that stands for its goods, `ledger.standingReceipt`), the invoice's, and the difference in euros. Posting an invoice is office's and the owner's: a billed line corrects its delivery-note line's price, a line received with it is received like a delivery note's. A credit note may bill a line again, its negative price a difference on the price now. An invoice isn't reversed while a later one that stands bills the same lines. A posted document's page says, for an invoice's line, the delivery-note line it billed, and for a delivery note's, the invoices that stand and bill it, in the order they were posted. `GET /api/products/:id/stock`, for office and the owner: a product's stock and average cost in each storeroom it has moved through, worked out by `ledger` from its movements (a database-level property test checks it). The seed adds four demo documents from the gold set, ready for review (three delivery notes, and the invoice for the first), with the suppliers and products they need, each row only if it isn't there, and checked as a read one is (decision 0039). Matching (#117, decision 0035): a document's matching is worked out when asked, never stored. It has its supplier, whether that supplier is new (fewer than 3 posted documents), each line's supplier product (by code, then by a description only one of the supplier's has) and why the document needs review. Office and owner choose a document's supplier and remember a line as a supplier product while it's in review, both audited with the document. When a document is read, a model suggests which of our products each line nothing matches is, once (decision 0036): the call and its answers are stored (`suggestion_calls`, `line_suggestions`), its cost counts in the document's, and a failed call leaves the lines without a suggestion. A suggestion shows in the matching answer, found by the line's code and description, until a person confirms it as a mapping. Corrections in review (#118, decision 0037): a document's draft is saved as corrected, whole, in its own column, apart from what the model read; saving runs the checks again, audited with the document, and a version can be checked without saving it. Everything after reading uses the corrected draft. Counter users may correct only a delivery note's quantities. A document's answer has its draft and the checks it failed by name and field, never a check's own message, which may quote the document; its reading cost goes only to the owner (spec section 6). `EXTRACTION_MODEL=fake` reads without OpenRouter, outside production; markers in a document make it fail, misread a quantity, or read the invoice for its delivery note. **Default model and prompt:** Gemini 3.8 Flash (decision 0018) with prompt v3 (decision 0040), at the provider's own temperature: the only zero-retention endpoints don't take one (decision 0031). They're set in `packages/extract/config/models.json` and read with `defaultExtraction()` (`src/llm/config.ts`). Suggesting products uses the same model with its own prompt, `prompts/suggestions/v1.md`, read with `defaultSuggestion()` (decision 0036). Prompts are versioned files in `packages/extract/prompts/`. Prompt v3 is v2 plus a rule asking for the delivery notes an invoice lists (`deliveryNotes` in the draft), which the Facturae reader takes from `DeliveryNotesReferences`; the eval reports that field without scoring it (decision 0040).

## The eval harness

- **Gold set:** `eval/gold/` has 20 committed documents: synthetic text PDFs, photos, scans and Facturae XML, each with a hand-checked `expected.json` and `meta.json`. `eval/gold/README.md` lists them. Runs over it use client routing, so they measure the endpoints staging and production reach (decision 0031). Its tests check the set's size, its metadata, that rendered PDFs match their source, and that no image carries metadata.
- **Private set:** client documents, found through `EVAL_PRIVATE_DIR`, never in the repo. Runs over it are started by the repo owner, always with restricted routing, and their results keep no value read.
- **`eval`:** runs a model and prompt over a set, with a spending cap. It writes `eval/results/<date>-<model>-<prompt>[-runN].json` and `.md`, and never overwrites a file (#68). The summary reports the silent error rate first, then the other section 5 metrics, and why any call failed (#64).
- **`eval:compare`:** runs side by side, with totals and ranges across repeated runs, and the documents whose outcome changed (#52, #68).
- **Scoring:** values are compared by meaning, not text, in `src/eval/normalize.ts`. A blank discount and 0% count as the same (decision 0019).

## The ledger

`packages/ledger/README.md` lists its modules and the property tests for each invariant in spec section 4. `src/invariants.test.ts` fails if an invariant loses its test, or if that README lists a test that doesn't exist. The numbers follow decisions 0010 (quantities) and 0015 (money as `bigint`). Costing follows decision 0005. An invoice corrects a delivery note's price with a receipt at its price plus the reversal of the old one, and costs count that receipt where the delivery note's was (decision 0032).

## Infrastructure

- **Hosting:** Render, Frankfurt (`render.yaml`, decision 0002).
  - **Staging:** web, an API on the free plan, and Postgres. Deployed from every commit on `main` once CI passes.
  - **Production:** web, API, Postgres with point-in-time recovery, and the weekly backup job. Deployed from a `v*` tag by `.github/workflows/deploy-production.yml`.
- **Database changes:** Drizzle migrations in `apps/api/drizzle/`, applied before each deploy takes traffic (decision 0004).
- **Monitoring:** Sentry for errors in both apps and an uptime check on production's `/health` (decision 0008).
- **Backups:** Render's point-in-time recovery, plus a weekly encrypted dump to Scaleway (`ops/backup/`, decision 0014). How to restore, and the drill record: `docs/runbooks/restore.md`.
- **CI** (`.github/workflows/ci.yml`), on every pull request and on `main`:
  - checks: no git-ignored file tracked, a secret scan, lint, format and typecheck;
  - tests, with Postgres;
  - database: that the migrations match the schema and apply to a fresh database, and the seed;
  - the API starting in production mode;
  - a build, and a backup and restore round trip.

How to run, deploy and operate all this is in the root `README.md`.
