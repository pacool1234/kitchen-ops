# Receiving a document

For everyone who receives deliveries: counter, office and owner. The app is in Spanish by default; the words on screen are given here in Spanish, with English in brackets.

## Sending it

1. Open **Documentos** (Documents) in the menu, then **Subir documento** (Upload a document).
2. **On a phone,** tap **Hacer foto** (Take a photo) once for each page of the delivery note or invoice, in order. Each photo appears numbered; remove one with its **×** button.
3. **On a computer,** drag the supplier's PDF or Facturae XML onto the page, or click **Elegir archivos** (Choose files). Photos can be chosen there too.
4. Tap **Enviar** (Send). The document's page opens.

What can be sent: up to 10 photos (JPEG or PNG), or one PDF, or one Facturae XML, each up to 20 MB. Other files, such as HEIC photos, are refused.

A file that was already sent is refused, with a link to the document that has it.

## While it's read

The document's status changes by itself, without reloading the page:

- **En cola** (Queued): waiting its turn.
- **Leyendo** (Reading): being read, usually within a minute.
- **Listo para revisar** (Ready for review): read. Its page shows what was read: the supplier, number, date, totals and lines, and any check it failed, such as a line whose amount doesn't match.
- **Ha fallado** (Failed): it couldn't be read, and its page says why. Tap **Reintentar** (Try again) to read it again.

Reviewing what was read, correcting it and posting it into stock: see [Reviewing a document and posting it into stock](reviewing-and-posting.md).

## For the owner

A document's page also shows what reading it cost, in US dollars: usually about a cent. No other role sees it.

## A new version of the app

When there's a new version, a bar at the top of the page says **Hay una versión nueva de la app** (There's a new version of the app). Tap **Recargar** (Reload) to use it now, or **Ahora no** (Not now). Otherwise it's used the next time you move to another page, after anything unsaved has been saved or left. An app left open notices a new version within half an hour, or when you bring it back to the front.
